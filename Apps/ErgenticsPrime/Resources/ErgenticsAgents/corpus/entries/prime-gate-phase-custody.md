Reusable decision: bind the current operation to its own authority, exact
revision, event/caller/phase predicates and observed checks. A workflow's event
surface, admission, CI result, native qualification, remote durability and
merge destination are different claims. Read both YAML and its immediate caller.

When the gate belongs to a prior phase, inspect its retained restoration or
transition contract. Preserve the failure. A proposed relaxation of protected
admission after an outcome is a protocol amendment for applicable review; an
already authorized restoration has its own conditions. Ordinary CI work is not
automatically a governed scientific attempt, and a scientific no-retry rule is
not blanket permission or a universal ban for unrelated tasks.

Golden negative: a correctly triggered, mergeable PR may fail caller admission.
Even a green static check does not establish native qualification or a main
merge. Benign control: with current user authorization, source inspection,
ordinary edits, metadata checks and bounded lesson packaging proceed while a
separate native execution prerequisite remains unmet. State the held operation.
